About us

BK CatStore is the home of 30 cats, each with their own personality and story. BK CatStore started with a simple idea: make it easy for people to find a cat that truly fits their life. Whether you're a first-time owner or a lifelong cat person, we're here to help you find the right match.

Every one of our 30 cats is well-cared for, unique, and ready to find their forever home. We take the time to get to know each of them so we can help you find the one that's right for you.

At BK CatStore, we're not just selling cats, we're making connections that last a lifetime.
